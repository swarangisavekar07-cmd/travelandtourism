## 3. Data Lineage & Provenance Details

1. **Originating Authority:** Market Research Division, Ministry of Tourism, Govt. of India.
2. **Official Source Documentation:**
   - PDF Publication: *India Tourism Statistics 2023* (Table 5.1.2, pp. 112–115)
   - PDF Publication: *India Tourism Data Compendium 2024* (Table 4.1.2, pp. 88–91)
   - Official Word Documents: *Tourism Statistics 2019, 2020, 2021*
3. **Historical Boundary Nuances Handled:**
   - **Ladakh Bifurcation:** Under the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019, Ladakh became a separate Union Territory on 31 October 2019. Prior to that date, figures were reported under Jammu & Kashmir. The pipeline strictly records Ladakh's 2018 visit count as `NULL`, preserving historical integrity.
   - **Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu:** Merged on 26 January 2020. Reported figures represent the unified constitutional territory.
   - **Ministry Growth Estimation:** Footnoted in official government tables that Maharashtra and Delhi 2022 domestic figures were estimated by the Ministry applying national 2022/2019 growth rates.
